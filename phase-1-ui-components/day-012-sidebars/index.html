Deploying

Deployments

Every push to your repository creates a deployment with its own URL.

How deployments work

Atlas watches your Git repository. Each commit triggers a build in an isolated environment, and the output is uploaded to our edge network in under a minute.

Builds are cached between runs, so unchanged dependencies are never downloaded twice.

Preview deployments

Every branch and pull request gets a preview URL. Share it with your team or client for feedback before anything goes live.

Previews are protected by default and expire after 30 days of inactivity.

Promoting to production

Merging to your production branch promotes the latest build instantly. There's no rebuild, because the exact preview your team approved goes live.

Instant rollback

Something wrong? Choose any previous deployment and promote it. Traffic switches in seconds, with no build needed.
