Day 009 / 100 Phase 1 · UI Components

Image Galleries

Six ways to show visual work: a filterable portfolio, a restaurant menu wall, a property photo viewer, a before/after slider, swipeable screenshots and a jewellery zoom. All of them share one accessible lightbox that supports the keyboard, swiping and screen readers.

  • 6gallery types
  • 1shared lightbox
  • Swipe& keyboard
  • 152 KBfor all 40 images

01

Halden · Filterable portfolio

A masonry portfolio for agencies, photographers and artists. Category filters rearrange the grid with a smooth animation, and any piece opens in a full-screen lightbox. You can move through the work with the arrow keys or a swipe.

  • Animated filtering
  • Lightbox
  • Swipe + keys

Ember · Menu photo wall

A bento-style grid of dishes for restaurants, cafés and bakeries. Hovering (or focusing) a dish reveals its name, description and price, a small heart lets guests save favourites, and every photo opens in the lightbox.

  • Hover captions
  • Save favourites

Forma · Property photo viewer

The listing gallery buyers expect: a large main photo with arrows and a counter, a thumbnail strip that follows along, room labels, and a “Show all photos” button that opens every photo full screen. You can swipe on phones and use the arrow keys on desktop.

Nova · Swipeable screenshots

Product screenshots in a smooth horizontal strip that snaps to each slide. It swipes on touch and click-and-drags with a mouse, with dots and a progress bar that show where you are. It's made for app and SaaS feature tours.

Aurum · Jewellery zoom viewer

For jewellery, watches and anything where detail sells. Hovering over the product shows a magnifying lens. Metal and stone swatches swap the photo instantly, and the price and name update with the choice. On touch screens, tapping opens the full-screen view.
