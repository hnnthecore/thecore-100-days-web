Day 007 / 100 Phase 1 · UI Components

Testimonials & Reviews

People trust people. These six sections turn customer voices into proof: walls of praise, logo carousels, filterable star ratings, results-driven case studies, member stories and a form for guests to leave their own review.

  • Filtersort & rate
  • Accessiblecarousel

A masonry wall of short, genuine-sounding quotes, the kind SaaS companies use to show momentum. Visitors can filter by role to find people like them, long quotes expand in place, and “Show more” reveals the rest without leaving the page.
