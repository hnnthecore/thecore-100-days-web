Day 019 / 100 Phase 1 · UI Components

Calendars & date pickers

Six ways to pick and show dates: a fully keyboard-accessible date picker, a hotel-style range picker, a team month calendar, a week schedule, a fare calendar and an “add to calendar” event card. All built on plain JavaScript dates, with no library.

  • 6calendar patterns
  • Keyboardgrid navigation
  • Ranges& presets
  • .icsexport

01

Atlas · Accessible date picker

Type a date, or open the calendar. It follows the WAI-ARIA date picker pattern: arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up/Down by month (Shift for year), Home/End to the week's ends, and Enter picks. Past dates and weekends can be blocked.

  • Type or pick
  • Full keyboard grid
  • Disabled dates
  • Smart parsing
atlas.dev/projects/new

New project

Weekdays only, from tomorrow. Type a date or use the calendar.

Type “next friday”, “tomorrow” or “24/12”, or press the calendar button and use the arrow keys.

02

Wander · Date range picker

The hotel-style check-in / check-out picker: two months side by side, the range previews as you hover, the nights are counted for you, and presets fill common ranges in one click. Booked-out days can't be inside a stay.

  • Two months
  • Hover preview
  • Presets
  • Blocked dates

03

Lumen · Team month calendar

The calendar view of an app. Colour-coded events, “+2 more” when a day is busy, today highlighted, and quick add: click any day, type an event and press Enter. On phones it becomes a dot calendar with an agenda list.

  • Month grid
  • Quick add
  • Category filters
  • Agenda on mobile
app.lumen.app/calendar

Click an empty part of a day to add an event, untick a category, and switch to Mobile for the agenda view.

04

Halden · Week schedule

A week laid out by the hour, like Google or Outlook calendar. Weekdays get more room than the weekend. Events sit at their real times and lengths, overlapping meetings share the space, and a red line shows the current time. Click any event for its details.

  • Time grid
  • Overlap layout
  • Now line
  • Event details
halden.studio/schedule

Click an event for details (Esc closes). The red line marks the current time (shown between 08:00 and 19:00).

05

Nova Air · Fare calendar

“When is it cheapest?” Each day shows its fare, colour-coded from cheapest to most expensive, sold-out days are crossed out, and the lowest price of the month is starred. Pick a day to see the flights.

  • Price per day
  • Colour scale
  • Cheapest highlight
  • Sold-out days
