Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for up to 3 people, with 3 projects and 5 GB of storage.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You get every Pro feature for 14 days with no card required. At the end you can pick a plan or drop back to Starter automatically.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not happy within 30 days of any payment, contact us for a full refund. No forms, no questions.

Do you send VAT invoices?

Every payment creates a VAT invoice you can download from Settings → Billing. Add your VAT number there and future invoices include it.

Do you support SSO?

SAML single sign-on with Okta, Azure AD and Google Workspace is included on the Business plan.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) by default, encrypted at rest and in transit. Enterprise customers can choose US or UK hosting.

Does Lumen integrate with Slack?

Yes. Connect Slack in one click to get updates in channels and create tasks with a shortcut.

Is there a public API?

Yes, a REST API with typed SDKs for JavaScript and Python, plus webhooks for real-time events.

      Buyer's guide

      Buying a home, step by step

      1. 1

        Before you buy

        How much deposit do I need?

        Usually at least 5% of the price. Banks typically lend up to 80%, and the rest can be covered by a top-up loan.

        Should I get mortgage approval first?

        Yes. An agreement in principle shows sellers you're serious and tells you exactly what you can spend.

      2. 2

        Making an offer

        How do I know what to offer?

        Your agent will share recent sales nearby. Most homes in Copenhagen sell within 2–4% of the asking price.

        Is my offer legally binding?

        Not until both sides sign the purchase agreement. You also get a 6-day right to withdraw after signing.

      3. 3

        Moving in

        How long until I get the keys?

        Typically 2–3 months from signing, agreed with the seller in the purchase contract.

        What costs come on top of the price?

        Registration fees, legal advice and bank fees. Budget around 2–3% of the price, and we'll give you an exact estimate.
