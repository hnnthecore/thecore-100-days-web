The atelier

Five steps. Forty hours.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    We draw your design by hand, then model it in 3D so you can see every angle before anything is made.

  2. 02

    Cast

    Recycled 18ct gold or platinum is cast in the studio, never outsourced, never mass-produced.

  3. 03

    Set

    Each stone is set under a microscope. Prongs are cut to fit that one stone.

  4. 04

    Polish

    Seven stages of hand polishing, from matt to mirror. This part cannot be hurried.

  5. 05

    Certify

    Every diamond over 0.30 ct comes with a GIA report, and every piece is insured for its first year.
