Your pizza£10.00

48 hours. 90 seconds.

Great pizza is mostly patience. Here's what happens to every ball of dough before it reaches you.

  1. 0h

    Mix

    Caputo “00” flour, sea salt, a pinch of yeast and cold water. That’s all.

  2. 2h

    Rest

    The dough relaxes at room temperature so the gluten can line up.

  3. 24h

    Cold prove

    A day in the fridge builds flavour and those big, airy bubbles.

  4. 46h

    Ball

    Shaped by hand into 270 g balls, then left to wake up.

  5. 48h

    90 seconds

    Stretched by hand and blistered at 485 °C in our wood-fired oven.
