Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key safe code or a key with us. Every cleaner is DBS-checked, ID-verified and insured for £5m.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes: eco-certified products, microfibre cloths and a HEPA vacuum. If you prefer your own products, just tell us in the notes.

What if I’m not happy?

Tell us within 24 hours and we’ll send the team back to put it right, free. If it’s still not right, you get a refund.

How do I pay?

After the clean, by card. We never ask for payment upfront for regular cleans and there’s no contract.

Can I get the same cleaner every time?

Yes. On weekly and fortnightly plans we assign a regular cleaner and a back-up, so you’re never left without a clean.

Which areas do you cover?

Leeds, Bradford, York, Harrogate, Wakefield, Halifax, Sheffield and Greater Manchester. Enter your postcode in the quote tool to check.

Prefer a chat?

Leave your number and we’ll call you back within 15 minutes, 8 am to 8 pm.
