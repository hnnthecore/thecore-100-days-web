## The challenge

Seasonal peaks meant huge stock buys followed by long lulls. Callum worried constantly about the quiet months.

## What changed

A seasonal forecast shaped by last year’s data let the shop plan stock purchases around cash, not nerves.

## The result

Petal & Stem went through its second wedding season without touching an overdraft, and opened a workshop space in the quiet months.
