## The challenge

Harbourline carries fuel and wage costs weeks before customers pay. Late payers hit hard.

## What changed

Automatic reminders in a friendly tone, plus a forecast that factors in how late each customer really is, replaced a stressful weekly chase.

## The result

The business smoothed out its cash cycle and renegotiated better terms with its fuel supplier using the forecast as evidence.
