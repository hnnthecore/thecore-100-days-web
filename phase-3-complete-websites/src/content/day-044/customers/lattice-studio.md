## The challenge

Project-based income made Lattice feel unpredictable. Good months and quiet months made planning headcount feel like gambling.

## What changed

With scenarios, Dev modelled a lost client and a new hire side by side. The agency chose to hire in stages, protecting a six-month cushion.

## The result

Lattice grew by a third in a year and the partners now get a one-page cash report every Monday without anyone building it.
