## The challenge

Northbound sells to cafes on 30-day terms but pays flour suppliers in 14. Rosa tracked it on a spreadsheet that only she understood and that was always a week out of date.

## What changed

Fathom connected to the bank and invoicing system in an afternoon. The first forecast showed a pinch point in March that nobody had seen coming, and Rosa moved a delivery of ovens to avoid it.

## The result

A year later, Northbound has opened two new shops without borrowing, and Rosa spends Friday afternoons baking instead of reconciling.
