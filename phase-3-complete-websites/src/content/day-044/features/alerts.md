## What you get

Quiet, useful alerts: a forecast dip, a big invoice gone late, a bill that is higher than usual.

## How it works

Alerts compare today against your own history, so a pattern-breaking bill is flagged but a normal one is not.

## Why it matters

You find out while there is still time to act, not on the day the money runs out.
