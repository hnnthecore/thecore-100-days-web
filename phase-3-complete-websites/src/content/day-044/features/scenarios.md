## What you get

Duplicate your forecast, change anything and see the difference in cash, runway and lowest balance instantly.

## How it works

Scenarios are layers on top of your live numbers, so when reality changes the scenarios update with it. Nothing is a stale spreadsheet.

## Why it matters

Big decisions deserve better than a hunch. Know how a hire or a late payer changes your runway before you say yes.
