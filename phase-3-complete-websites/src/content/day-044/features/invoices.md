## What you get

A short list each morning of who to nudge, with a message ready to send in your voice.

## How it works

Reminders go out from your own email at the times your customers actually read them. Replies land in your inbox, not ours.

## Why it matters

Customers on Fathom are paid 9 days sooner on average, which is often the difference between a comfortable month and a stressful one.
