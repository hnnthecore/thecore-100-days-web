## What you will learn

By the end of your first ten-week course you will be able to introduce yourself, ask and answer everyday questions and understand the gist of a simple conversation in French. Each level builds on the last, from first words at A1 to confident discussion at B2.

## How a lesson works

Every lesson follows the same rhythm: a warm-up chat, a short new idea, plenty of pair practice and a final challenge where you use what you learned straight away. Homework is optional, short and always useful.

## Who it is for

Beginners, returners and travellers are all welcome. If you have studied French before, take our free level test and we will place you in the right group.
