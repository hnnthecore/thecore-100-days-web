## What it is

Professional whitening is safer and more effective than anything from a shop. It lifts years of tea, coffee and wine from your smile.

## What happens

We make custom trays for you to wear at home with a medical-grade gel, plus an in-surgery boost to kick things off.

## Afterwards

Avoid strongly coloured food and drink for 48 hours. Top up once or twice a year to keep your smile bright.
