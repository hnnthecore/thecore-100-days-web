## What it is

Good habits start early. Our team makes first visits fun, with stickers, stories and a very patient dentist.

## What happens

We count teeth, show children how to brush and apply protective fissure sealants where helpful.

## Afterwards

We recommend a first visit as soon as the first tooth appears, then every six months. Under-18s are free on the NHS-style plan.
