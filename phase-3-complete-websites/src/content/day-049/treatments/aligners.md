## What it is

Clear aligners gradually move your teeth into place using a series of removable trays. Most adults notice a difference within a few months.

## What happens

We scan your teeth, show you a 3D preview of your final smile and order a set of trays that you swap every two weeks.

## Afterwards

After treatment you will wear a retainer at night. We check your progress every six weeks along the way.
