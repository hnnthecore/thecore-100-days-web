## What it is

A check-up is the best way to catch problems while they are small. Your dentist will look at your teeth, gums and mouth, and talk through anything they find.

## What happens

We start with a gentle exam, take X-rays only when they are useful and finish with a polish that removes surface stains.

## Afterwards

Most people need a check-up every six to twelve months. We will tell you when to come back, and send a reminder.
