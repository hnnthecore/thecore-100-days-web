## What it is

Dental pain rarely waits. We keep emergency slots free every day so that you can be seen quickly.

## What happens

We find the cause, relieve the pain and give you a clear plan, whether that means a filling, a repair or a referral.

## Afterwards

If you have facial swelling or trouble swallowing or breathing, call 999 or go to A&E straight away.
