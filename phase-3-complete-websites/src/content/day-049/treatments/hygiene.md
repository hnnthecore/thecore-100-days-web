## What it is

Brushing cannot reach everything. A hygienist removes the hard tartar that builds up along the gum line and teaches you how to keep it away.

## What happens

Using gentle ultrasonic tools and a fine polish, we clean above and below the gum line. Most people find it relaxing.

## Afterwards

Gums may feel tender for a day. Brush gently, use the interdental brushes we show you and book a repeat in three to six months.
