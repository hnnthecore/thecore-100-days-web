## Brief

Affordable family homes at density, without losing the sense of a street where neighbours know each other.

## Approach

Terraced houses frame a shared courtyard with growing beds, play space and a bike barn. Every front door opens onto the garden.

## Outcome

A waiting list of more than 300, and a residents’ group that organises a street party every June.
