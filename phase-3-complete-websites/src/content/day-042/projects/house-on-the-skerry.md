## Brief

A retired couple wanted a home that felt sheltered in a storm and bright on the rare calm day, with room for six grandchildren.

## Approach

We folded the house into the slope so the roof reads as a stone. The living spaces face the water through one long window, while bedrooms sit behind thick walls on the leeward side.

## Outcome

The house uses 78% less energy than a typical new build, and the owners say the best moment is a winter storm from the window seat.
