## Corridors are classrooms

The corridors at Birch Court are four metres wide, with window seats and borrowed daylight. They are the school’s second classroom.

## Every class has a garden

Each classroom opens to a covered veranda and a growing bed. The weather becomes part of the curriculum.

## Listening to the children

We ran workshops with the pupils, who asked for three things: a place to hide, a place to be loud and a place to see the sky. All three made it into the plan.
