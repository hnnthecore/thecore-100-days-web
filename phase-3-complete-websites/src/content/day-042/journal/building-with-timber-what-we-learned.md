## 1. Detail for water first

Timber and water are an old argument. Every junction on our timber projects starts with the question: where does the rain go, and where does it dry?

## 2. Leave some of it showing

Exposed structure is cheaper, calmer and healthier than boarding everything over. The smell alone sells the idea.

## 3. Talk to the fire engineer on day one

Exposed timber can meet fire regulations, but only if the strategy is designed in from the start.

## 4. Prefabricate, but not everything

Factory-made floors and walls save weeks on site. Details that depend on the ground should stay on site.

## 5. Carbon is the argument, comfort is the pleasure

The carbon story gets timber on the shortlist. The warmth, light and quiet are why clients stay in love with it.
