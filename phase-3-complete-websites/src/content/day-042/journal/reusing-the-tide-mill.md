## Week 1: stand and look

We spent the first week simply standing in the building. The mill had been shaped by 200 years of work, and most of that shape was worth keeping.

## Week 6: the stone

A mason sorted the fallen masonry into three piles: reuse as it is, reuse recut and recycle. In the end 70% went back into the building.

## Week 30: the wheel

The day the waterwheel turned again, half the village came to watch. Nobody had planned for that, and it was the best day of the project.
