## Not hot, just even

In a Passivhaus there are no draughts and no cold spots. The temperature is the same by the window as by the sofa.

## Fresh air, all the time

Mechanical ventilation brings filtered air in continuously, which means no condensation, no mould and no stuffiness.

## The bills

The heating bill for a family home is typically about the cost of running a hairdryer, which is the number clients tell their friends.
