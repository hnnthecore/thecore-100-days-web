## The leap

Dev had never grown anything before. In week two of the course he planted a row of onions in the wrong direction. He now laughs about it.

## Eight weeks later

He finished the course top of the class, did a placement at a local nursery and was offered a job there. He turned it down to come back to Rootwork.

## What is next

He is training the next cohort and has a plan to double our winter salad crop. We believe him.
