## A bumper year

We planted sixty tomato plants in March and hoped for the best. By August the greenhouse looked like a jungle, and the picking rota was fully booked.

## Where they all went

Roughly a third went to the community fridge, a third into the Thursday cooking sessions and a third into veg boxes for families who needed them most.

## What we learned

Pick early, pick often, and always make more chutney than you think you could possibly need.
