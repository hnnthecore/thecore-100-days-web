## What happens

A paid, accredited course in propagation, soil, planting plans and market growing, with weekly work placements at local nurseries and landscaping firms.

## Who it is for

Adults looking for a change of direction or a first step into work. No experience needed. Childcare costs are covered.

## What you take home

A Level 2 horticulture certificate, a reference and, for 8 in 10 trainees, a job offer within three months.
