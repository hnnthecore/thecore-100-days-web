Spiced apple crumble

The classic pudding, with a little cinnamon and a lot of crunch.

55 minServes 6Easy410 kcal per servingBritish

Ingredients

Serves 6

    Tick what you already have, then add the rest to your list.

    The classic pudding, with a little cinnamon and a lot of crunch. This recipe serves 6 and takes about 55 minutes from start to finish.

    Method

    1. Heat the oven to 190C. Peel and slice the apples and toss with sugar and cinnamon in a baking dish.
    2. Rub the cold butter into the flour until it looks like breadcrumbs, then stir in the oats and a spoon of sugar.
    3. Scatter the crumble over the apples without pressing it down.
    4. Bake until bubbling at the edges and golden on top.40 min
    5. Rest for 10 minutes, then serve with custard or ice cream.10 min

    Cook's tip: Add a handful of blackberries for colour and a gentle tartness.
