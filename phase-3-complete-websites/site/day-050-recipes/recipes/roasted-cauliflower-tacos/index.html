Roasted cauliflower tacos

Charred, spicy and piled high with crunchy slaw.

40 minServes 4Easy350 kcal per servingMexican

Ingredients

Serves 4

    Tick what you already have, then add the rest to your list.

    Charred, spicy and piled high with crunchy slaw. This recipe serves 4 and takes about 40 minutes from start to finish.

    Method

    1. Heat the oven to 220C. Toss cauliflower florets with oil, cumin and salt, then roast until charred at the edges.30 min
    2. Shred the cabbage and toss with the juice of one lime and a pinch of salt.
    3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 20 seconds on each side.2 min
    4. Fill with cauliflower and slaw and finish with coriander and lime wedges.

    Cook's tip: A spoon of tahini thinned with lime juice makes a great drizzle.
