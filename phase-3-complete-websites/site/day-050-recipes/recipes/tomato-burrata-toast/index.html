Burrata and tomato toast

Summer on a slice, no cooking required.

10 minServes 2Easy410 kcal per servingItalian

Ingredients

Serves 2

    Tick what you already have, then add the rest to your list.

    Summer on a slice, no cooking required. This recipe serves 2 and takes about 10 minutes from start to finish.

    Method

    1. Toast the sourdough until golden and rub one side with a cut garlic clove if you like.3 min
    2. Chop the tomatoes and toss with oil, balsamic and a good pinch of salt.
    3. Pile the tomatoes on the toast and tear the burrata over the top.
    4. Scatter with basil and finish with a grind of black pepper.

    Cook's tip: Leave the tomatoes to sit for ten minutes. They release their sweet juices.
