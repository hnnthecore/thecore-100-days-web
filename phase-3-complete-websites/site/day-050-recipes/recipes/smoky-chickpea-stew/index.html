Smoky chickpea stew

A warming one-pot dinner that tastes even better tomorrow.

35 minServes 4Easy380 kcal per servingSpanish

Ingredients

Serves 4

    Tick what you already have, then add the rest to your list.

    A warming one-pot dinner that tastes even better tomorrow. This recipe serves 4 and takes about 35 minutes from start to finish.

    Method

    1. Soften the chopped onion in the oil over a medium heat for 8 minutes.8 min
    2. Add the garlic and paprika and stir for a minute until fragrant.1 min
    3. Tip in the tomatoes and drained chickpeas with a splash of water. Simmer gently.20 min
    4. Stir through the spinach until wilted, then season well and serve with crusty bread.2 min

    Cook's tip: Finish with a squeeze of lemon to wake all the flavours up.
