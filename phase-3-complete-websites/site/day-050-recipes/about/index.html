Recipes without the scroll.

We believe a recipe should be short, honest and a pleasure to cook from.

Citrus & Salt started in a small flat kitchen where the cook was tired of scrolling past three pages of backstory to find the ingredients. So we built the site we wanted: ingredients first, clear steps, and tools that help.

Every recipe is tested at least three times, costed honestly and written so a beginner can follow it without asking anyone.

  • Scaling

    Amounts are stored as numbers, not text, so doubling a recipe is simple multiplication. Results are rounded to kitchen fractions like ½ and ¾.

  • Units

    Grams and millilitres convert to ounces and fluid ounces with one tap. Spoon measures stay the same in both.

  • Cook mode

    A full-screen view with one step at a time, large type and timers. Arrow keys move between steps.

  • Shopping list

    Items with the same name and unit are merged. Your list and favourites live in your browser, never on a server.
