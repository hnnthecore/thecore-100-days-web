Why we still draw by hand first

By Elin Sørensen18 September 20261 min read

In this article

  1. The first hour
  2. What the drawings are for
  3. Then, and only then, the model

Before any software opens, every project starts with a pencil. Here is why that slow first hour pays for itself.

The first hour

On every project, the first hour is spent with tracing paper and a soft pencil. There is no model, no grid and no programme of accommodation yet, only the site and a question.

A pencil is slow and imprecise in a way that is useful. It forces you to decide what matters and leave out what doesn’t.

What the drawings are for

These early sketches are not meant to be beautiful. They are meant to be argued with. We pin them to the wall, walk past them for a week and notice which lines we keep redrawing.

Those are the lines the building is really about.

Then, and only then, the model

By the time the computer comes out, the big ideas are settled. The software’s job is to test them, not to invent them.
