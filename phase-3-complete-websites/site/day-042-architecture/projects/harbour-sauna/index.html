Harbour Sauna

Brief

A community sauna on the harbour wall for 12 people, built from local timber and using no mains power.

Approach

The building is two rooms and a veranda. Charred larch resists the salt, and the big window frames the boats coming home.

Outcome

Members have grown from 40 to 600 in a year, and the sauna has its own waiting list.
