The Orchard Pavilion

An open-sided pavilion for weddings, workshops and winter apple pressing.

Brief

A flexible shelter in an old orchard that is lovely in July and still useful in January.

Approach

A broad timber roof floats on slender columns above a rammed-earth floor. Sliding screens close the sides when the weather demands it.

Outcome

Booked solid from May to September, and the trust now presses 8 tonnes of apples a year under it.
