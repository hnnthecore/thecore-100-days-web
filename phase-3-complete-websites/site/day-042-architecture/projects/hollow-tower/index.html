The site, Glasgow. Position is approximate.

Brief

A speculative office building that could attract tenants who care about wellbeing and carbon, on a narrow city plot.

Approach

We hollowed out the middle of the floor plate into a vertical garden, so every desk is within eight metres of a window and a leaf.

Outcome

Fully let before completion, and rated BREEAM Outstanding.
