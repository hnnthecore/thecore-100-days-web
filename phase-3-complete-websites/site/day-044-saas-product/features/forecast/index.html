See 13 weeks ahead, updated every morning

Fathom reads your bank, invoices and bills overnight and rebuilds a 13-week cash forecast before you have had your coffee.

Try Rolling forecastSee it live

What you get

A single line that shows your cash balance for every week ahead, with the lowest point flagged so you never get surprised by payroll again.

How it works

We combine what is already in the bank with invoices due, bills scheduled and your usual patterns, then learn from how late your customers really pay rather than when they are supposed to.

Why it matters

Most small businesses fail from running out of cash, not from losing money. A forecast turns a nasty surprise into a Tuesday-morning decision.
