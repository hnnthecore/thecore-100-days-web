Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Something missing? Talk to a human.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of the Growth plan with no card needed. At the end you pick a plan or your account simply pauses; nothing is charged by surprise.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades start at your next billing date.

What does annual billing save me?

Paying annually takes 20% off every plan, which is the equivalent of two months free.

Is my bank data safe?

Fathom uses read-only open banking connections, so we can see transactions but never move money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Do you offer discounts for charities?

Yes. Registered charities get 30% off any plan. Get in touch and we will set it up.
