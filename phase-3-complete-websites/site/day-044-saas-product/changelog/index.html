What's new.

We ship every two weeks. Here is everything that has changed, newest first.

  1. v4.2New24 September 2026

    Scenarios can now be shared

    Send any scenario to your accountant as a private link. They can comment but not change your numbers.

  2. v4.1Improved10 September 2026

    Forecasts load twice as fast

    We rebuilt how weekly totals are calculated. Large accounts with 50,000 transactions now open in under a second.

  3. v4.0New27 August 2026

    Multi-currency forecasting

    Hold balances in GBP, EUR and USD and see everything in your reporting currency, with live exchange rates.

  4. v3.9Fixed12 August 2026

    Fixed duplicate bank transactions

    A small number of customers saw the same transaction twice after a bank reconnect. This is fixed and duplicates are cleaned up automatically.

  5. v3.8New29 July 2026

    Slack alerts

    Get low-balance and late-payer alerts in the channel of your choice, with a one-click snooze.

  6. v3.7Improved15 July 2026

    Smarter late-payer predictions

    The model now learns from seasonality, so December payers are no longer treated like July payers.

  7. v3.6New30 June 2026

    Investor report templates

    Three new board-pack templates with runway, burn and cash conversion charts, ready for PDF export.

  8. v3.5Fixed11 June 2026

    Accessibility improvements

    Charts now have keyboard navigation and text summaries, and every control meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
