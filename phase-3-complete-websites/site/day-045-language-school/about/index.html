A school that talks back.

Founded in 2012 by two friends who were tired of silent classrooms. Today over 3,000 students have learned to speak with us.

What we believe

  • 1

    Speak first

    Grammar matters, but talking matters more. Every lesson is at least half conversation.

  • 2

    Small and friendly

    Never more than eight students, so nobody hides and everybody improves.

  • 3

    Real situations

    Menus, trains, job interviews and gossip. We teach the language you will actually use.

  • 4

    Teachers, not apps

    Apps are great for practice. Humans are better for confidence, feedback and laughs.

FAQ

Questions we often hear.

Do I need any experience?

No. Our A1 courses start from zero. If you have studied before, take the free level test and we will place you in the right group.

Are classes online or in person?

Both. Every course runs in our London studio and live online, and you can switch between them week to week.

What if I miss a lesson?

Every lesson is recorded for you to catch up, and your teacher will send a short summary of what you missed.

Can I try before I commit?

Yes. Your first lesson is free: if you do not love it, we refund the course in full.

Do you prepare for exams?

Yes, we prepare students for DELE, DELF, Goethe, JLPT, HSK and more, as part of B1 and B2 courses.
