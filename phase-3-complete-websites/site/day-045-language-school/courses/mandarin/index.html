你好

Mandarin classes in London and online

The world’s most spoken language, made approachable. 1.1 billion speakers.

Mandarin is the key to business and culture across Asia. We teach tones through songs and games, and characters through stories.

  • Tone training that sticks
  • Pinyin first, characters step by step
  • HSK exam preparation

What you will learn

By the end of your first ten-week course you will be able to introduce yourself, ask and answer everyday questions and understand the gist of a simple conversation in Mandarin. Each level builds on the last, from first words at A1 to confident discussion at B2.

How a lesson works

Every lesson follows the same rhythm: a warm-up chat, a short new idea, plenty of pair practice and a final challenge where you use what you learned straight away. Homework is optional, short and always useful.

Who it is for

Beginners, returners and travellers are all welcome. If you have studied Mandarin before, take our free level test and we will place you in the right group.

Syllabus

From first words to real fluency.

Four levels, ten weeks each. Pick a step to see what you will be able to do, week by week.

A1 · Beginner

No Mandarin needed. By the end you can handle first conversations and simple everyday situations.

Enrol at A1 · £189
  1. Week 1Say hello, goodbye and introduce yourself
  2. Week 2Count to 100 and give your phone number
  3. Week 3Order a drink and a snack
  4. Week 4Talk about your family and friends
  5. Week 5Tell the time and make plans
  6. Week 6Describe where you live
  7. Week 7Ask for and give simple directions
  8. Week 8Shop for food and clothes
  9. Week 9Talk about your daily routine
  10. Week 10Mini-challenge: a five-minute conversation in Mandarin

A2 · Elementary

You know the basics. Now you will talk about the past, the future and the things you like.

Enrol at A2 · £189
  1. Week 1Describe what you did at the weekend
  2. Week 2Talk about your job or studies
  3. Week 3Make and accept invitations
  4. Week 4Book a hotel or a table
  5. Week 5Describe people and places in detail
  6. Week 6Talk about health and see a pharmacist
  7. Week 7Compare things and give simple opinions
  8. Week 8Plan a trip and buy tickets
  9. Week 9Tell a short story about your past
  10. Week 10Mini-challenge: plan a day out together in Mandarin

B1 · Intermediate

You can cope with most situations. Weeks are built around real conversations and listening.

Enrol at B1 · £189

  2. Week 2Follow a short news story

  4. Week 4Solve problems on the phone

  7. Week 7Talk about hypothetical situations

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Fluent, natural and spontaneous. You argue a point, understand native speed and write with confidence.
