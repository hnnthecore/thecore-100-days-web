About this product

Thyme grows in the dry stone wall behind the barn, and the bees go straight to it in June. We steep the sprigs in raw honey from the next valley for three weeks and strain it clear.

A savoury, floral honey that is good on toast, in hot lemon, and spooned over goat cheese.

Ingredients

Raw honey, fresh thyme (Thymus vulgaris).

How to use

Straight from the jar, or stirred into tea.

Good to know

Contains honey. Not suitable for children under 12 months.
