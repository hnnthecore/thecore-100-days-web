Root canal treatment

Saves a badly damaged tooth and ends the pain.

What it is

A root canal has an unfair reputation. In reality it removes the painful infection inside a tooth and lets you keep it.

What happens

Under local anaesthetic, we clean the inside of the tooth, seal it and protect it with a filling or crown.

Afterwards

Mild tenderness for a few days is normal. Painkillers from the pharmacy are usually enough, and most people feel better immediately.
