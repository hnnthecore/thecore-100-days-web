Dental implants

A permanent, natural-looking replacement for a missing tooth.

What it is

An implant replaces both the root and the crown, so it looks, feels and works like a real tooth. With care, it can last a lifetime.

What happens

A small titanium post is placed in the jaw under local anaesthetic. After it has healed, a custom crown is fitted on top.

Afterwards

Healing takes three to six months. You will have regular reviews, and we show you how to keep the area spotless.
