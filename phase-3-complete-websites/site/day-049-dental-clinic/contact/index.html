Getting here

  • By trainLeeds station is a five-minute walk. Come out of the main entrance and head up towards City Square, then Park Row is on your left.
  • By busMost buses into the centre stop within a couple of minutes’ walk. Tell us which you are taking when you book and we will say where to get off.
  • By car or bikePay-and-display parking is close by, and there are cycle stands outside. If parking is the worry, ring us and we will help.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We welcome adults and children, and a first consultation for new patients is free.

Do you treat nervous patients?

Every day. Tell us at booking and we will give you extra time, a calm room and the pace you choose.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Treatment plans over £300 can be spread over 6 to 24 months, interest free.

What if I need to cancel?

Free of charge up to 24 hours before. Otherwise, a small fee may apply for longer appointments.

Is there step-free access?

Yes. The clinic is on the ground floor with a ramp, an accessible toilet and a hearing loop.
