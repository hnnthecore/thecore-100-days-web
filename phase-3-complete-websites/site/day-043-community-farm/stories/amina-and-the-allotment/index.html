Amina and the allotment

Rootwork team12 September 20261 min read

Amina arrived at the farm six months ago, new to the city and not sure where to start. Today she runs the Saturday seedling table.

A cold first Saturday

Amina had moved to Manchester in February and knew almost nobody. A neighbour mentioned the farm, and she turned up in the rain with the wrong shoes.

Somebody handed her a mug of tea and a trowel, and she has not really stopped since.

Finding a job to do

By April she had taken over the seedling table. She knows the name of every tray, which ones need more light, and which ones the slugs will go for first.

What she says now

I came for something to do. I stayed because people noticed when I was not there.
