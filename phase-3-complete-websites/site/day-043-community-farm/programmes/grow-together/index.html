Grow Together

Community growing for everyone

When
Tuesdays and Saturdays
Who it's for
All ages
340neighbours growing food each week

What happens

Twice a week the farm opens its gates. You can plant, weed, harvest or simply drink tea in the sun. Every session has a grower on hand to teach, and every bed is shared.

Who it is for

Anyone, whether you have grown before or have never touched a trowel. Many of our regulars first came for the company and stayed for the courgettes.

What you take home

A share of whatever is ripe, a few new skills and, for many people, a new group of friends. Surplus goes to the community fridge on Wilmslow Road.

What to expect

A day at the farm

Tuesdays and Saturdays. Open March to October.

  1. 10:00

    Open the gates

    Kettle on, coffee out, sign in with a first name only.

  2. 10:30

    Choose a job

    Weeding, watering, sowing, composting. No experience needed.

  3. 12:00

    Shared lunch

    Whatever came out of the ground, plus whatever people brought.

  4. 13:00

    Take-home bag

    Fill a bag from the harvest table. Pay what you can, or nothing.

  5. 14:00

    Close up

    Tools cleaned and counted, gates locked.
