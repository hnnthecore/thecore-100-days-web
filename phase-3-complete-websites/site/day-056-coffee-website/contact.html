How should I serve cold brew?

Chill well, shake gently and pour straight or over ice. Add milk if that is your ritual.

Is the coffee sweetened?

Black Current and Classic Nitro contain no added sugar. Flavoured recipes vary; final production labels would carry full ingredient and allergen information.
