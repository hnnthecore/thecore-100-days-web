The short story

Good coffee should feel simple

Our first batches were steeped overnight for friends who wanted café-quality coffee before the cafés opened. That practical idea still guides every choice we make.

01 · Source

Coffee with a clear origin

We buy seasonal lots for sweetness, structure and traceability. Each roast is selected to stay expressive after a long, cool extraction.

02 · Steep

Time does the work

Cold water draws out chocolate, fruit and caramel notes without the sharp edge. We taste every batch before it reaches the can.

The result

Full flavour. Clean finish. Ready when you are.
