Cookies.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

We do not use cookies

This website does not set any cookies. There are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no third-party tracking.

What your browser remembers

To make the timetable work, the site stores one small list in your browser's local storage, under the name stillwater-bookings. It holds the classes you have booked on this device, so that the “Booked” count and “My classes” stay correct from page to page. It is not sent anywhere.

How to clear it

Cancel a class from “My classes” and it is removed from the list. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
