Privacy.

This page explains what Stillwater Studio Ltd does with your details. We are at 9 Kensington Gardens, Brighton BN1 4AL. Last updated 24 October 2026.

What we ask for, and why

When you book a class we ask for your name and email so we can hold your place, send a confirmation and tell you if a class changes. When you write to us through the contact form we use your name, email and message to reply. If you tell us about an injury or a pregnancy, that note goes to your teacher and nobody else.

What we do not do

We do not sell your details, share them for advertising, or send marketing you did not ask for. We do not run advertising or tracking tools on this website.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for twelve months after the class so we can answer questions about a visit or a payment. Messages are kept for twelve months. After that they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. Email hello@stillwateryoga.co.uk and we will answer within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handled something, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

On this device

The timetable remembers the classes you book in your browser's own storage so that “Booked” shows up on every page. See the cookies page for exactly what is stored.
