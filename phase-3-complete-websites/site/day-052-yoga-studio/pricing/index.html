How often will you practise?

    Good to know.

    Do I need to bring anything?

    Just yourself and something comfortable. Mats, blocks, straps and blankets are all here for you.

    Can I freeze my membership?

    Yes. Pause for up to two months a year, free of charge, for travel, injury or life.

    Do passes expire?

    A 10-class pass is valid for six months, so you can practise at your own rhythm.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Cancel free up to two hours before class. After that, the class counts as used.

    Is there a student or concession rate?

    Yes. Students, over-65s and NHS staff get 20% off any pass or membership.
