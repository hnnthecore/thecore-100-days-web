Good to know

Questions we often hear

Can I order a book you don’t stock?

Yes. Order before 4 pm and it is usually here the next working day. We’ll text you when it arrives.

Do you buy secondhand books?

We do, on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Bring up to twenty books and we’ll give you an offer on the spot, in cash or shop credit.

Is the shop accessible?

The ground floor is step-free with wide aisles. The upstairs room has a stairlift, and events can be moved downstairs on request.

Do you do gift cards?

Yes, in store and by email. Gift wrapping is always free.

Can I bring my dog?

Of course. Water bowl by the door, biscuits behind the till.
