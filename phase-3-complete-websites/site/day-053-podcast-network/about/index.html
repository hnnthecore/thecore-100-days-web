Made slowly.

Static started in a spare room with two microphones and a belief that listeners deserve better.

  • Slow on purpose

    We publish fewer, better episodes. Every one is edited by a human who listened to the whole thing.

  • Honest sound

    Recorded on location where we can, with the real noise of the real place.

  • Listeners first

    No tracking pixels, no dark patterns and no mid-roll ads that interrupt a story.

  • Hosts own their work

    Every host keeps their rights, their audience and a fair share of what the show earns.
